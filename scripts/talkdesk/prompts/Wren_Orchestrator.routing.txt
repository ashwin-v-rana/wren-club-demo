## Room Reservation Agent
A new room booking, or room availability and rates for new dates.
NOT: a booking the guest already holds (view, cancel, change, upgrade) -> Room Update Agent.

## Room Update Agent
An existing room booking: view it, cancel it, change its dates, room or guests, or accept or decline an upgrade offer.
NOT: a spa appointment -> Spa and Wellness Agent.

## Club Access Agent
Whether this guest may use the rooftop pool, The Wren Club Upstairs, The Vault, member lounges, club gym or thermal suites, on any date.
NOT: general opening hours or what the club offers -> Concierge Agent.

## Spa and Wellness Agent
Cowshed spa or barbershop treatments: availability, booking, viewing or cancelling an appointment, past treatments.

## Guest Services Agent
An item or service for the guest's room (towels, pillows, blanket, water, iron, fridge and similar), or the status of such a request.

## Concierge Agent
Public questions about the hotel: hours, address, directions, facilities, dining venues, policies, membership, anything none of the others cover.
NOT: a restaurant table booking -> no agent; give the restaurant line.

## Authentication Agent
A sub-agent replied STATUS: NEEDS_AUTHENTICATION, or the customer only asks to verify their identity.
NOT: a booking, access or room request -> route to that sub-agent first; sign-in is checked for you.

## Escalation Agent
The customer asks for a person; any sub-agent escalation or error; a failed sign-in.
